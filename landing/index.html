      Option+Tab

      Cmd+Tab knows apps. You work in windows.

      Two Safari windows hide behind one Safari icon in Cmd+Tab. In DogBar’s switcher, every window gets its own tile with its name, so you land on the one you meant. Hold Option, tap Tab, let go.

      Preview before you switch.

      Hover an app in the Dock to see each of its windows. Click one and only that window comes forward. Close, minimize or go full screen from the preview.

      Each desktop’s Dock shows the apps with windows there, so trip planning and your weekend stay apart. Pinned apps stay on every Dock.

      Count windows at a glance.

      One dot for every open window, like the Windows taskbar. Drag the Dock’s edge to resize it. Icons stay put, with no magnification.

      Apple has not notarized this preview. Open the DMG and drag DogBar to Applications. If macOS blocks opening it, review the app and use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Then grant Accessibility and Screen Recording when requested.

      Questions

      Do I have to replace Apple’s Dock?

      No. Native Dock mode keeps Apple’s Dock and adds window previews and the Option+Tab switcher.

      Why does DogBar need Accessibility and Screen Recording?

      Accessibility lets it focus, close and minimize individual windows and handle its keyboard shortcut. Screen Recording lets it draw the window thumbnails in previews and the switcher.

      How do I get my normal Dock back?

      Click the paw in the menu bar and choose Pause DogBar or Restore Native Dock. Both bring back your original Dock settings.

      Which keys does the switcher use?

      Hold Option and tap Tab to move forward. Add Shift to go back, use the arrow keys to move around, and press Escape to cancel.
