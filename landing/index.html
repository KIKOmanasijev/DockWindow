      Alt-tab, done right

      Alt-tab to any window, not just any app.

      On a Mac, Cmd+Tab shows one icon per app, so two Safari windows look like one. DogBar gives every window its own tile, with its name and a preview. Hold Option, tap Tab, let go.

      Hover the Dock to preview windows.

      Hover any app to see all its windows. Click the one you want and only that window comes up. You can close or minimize it right from the preview.

      Get a separate Dock on every desktop.

      Each desktop’s Dock only shows the apps open there, so work and personal stuff stay apart. Your pinned apps show up everywhere.

      See how many windows each app has.

      Each app gets one dot per open window. Want a bigger Dock? Drag its edge. Icons stay still, with no magnifying.

      Rather keep Apple’s Dock?

      Turn on Native Dock mode. Apple’s Dock stays, and you still get window previews and the better alt-tab. A Dock per desktop and window dots need DogBar’s own Dock. Pause DogBar anytime to get your old Dock back.

      You can also choose whether the Dock, alt-tab and previews show windows from this desktop or from all of them.

      Questions

      Do I have to replace Apple’s Dock?

      No. Native Dock mode keeps Apple’s Dock and still adds window previews and the better alt-tab.

      Why does DogBar need Accessibility and Screen Recording?

      Accessibility lets it bring up, close and minimize single windows, and listen for its shortcut. Screen Recording lets it show what’s inside each window in the previews.

      How do I get my normal Dock back?

      Click the paw in the menu bar and choose Pause DogBar or Restore Native Dock. Either one brings your old Dock back.

      How do I alt-tab with DogBar?

      Hold Option and tap Tab. Add Shift to go back, or use the arrow keys. Let go of Option to switch, or press Escape to cancel.
