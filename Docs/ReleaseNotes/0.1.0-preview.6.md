*Unnotarized, self-signed preview for Apple Silicon Macs on macOS 26 or later.*

- **In-app updates (Sparkle 2).** Check for Updates downloads, verifies and installs newer previews after you choose Install and Relaunch. The update feed and every update are signed with DogBar's own update key. Settings > General > Updates turns daily background checks on or off. Nothing installs without your confirmation.
- Each desktop has its own Dock pins and ordering.
- Previews, the switcher and the Dock share the native glass appearance.
- Preview and New Window tile hover follows the cursor reliably, and tile sizing is fixed.
- DogBar asks before sending anonymous usage data. It is off unless you opt in.
- Dock rendering stays out of native menu tracking, and desktop labels follow the live desktop.
